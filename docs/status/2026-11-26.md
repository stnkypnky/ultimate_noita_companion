# 2026-11-26: Bestiary parts and the Hiisi toxic allergy

The user beat Kolmisilmä and saw its bestiary animation had no arms. They asked for an audit of every creature whose look depends on extra sprites. They also pointed out that Toxic sludge was in no creature's weaknesses, even though notes called some creatures weak to it.

## Missing parts
An audit compared every creature's child entities with `enemies.json`.
- **Sheet-drawn IK limbs.** `legsOf()` only took `.png` segments.
  - It now crops the first frame of `<Sprite>` sheets with `firstFrame()` in `tools/extract/enemies.ts`. That restores Kolmisilmä's 10 arms (80×16, reach 160) and Kolmisilmän sydän's 12 legs.
  - A creature that flies and can't walk (PathFinding `can_fly=1 can_walk=0`) clings to the walls around it. This covers boss_centipede, boss_meat, boss_limbs and boss_robot.
  - `LimbBossComponent` was tried as the cling signal first, but every physics-legged creature has it.
- **Verlet chains** (`VerletPhysicsComponent` children) were never drawn. They are now `Enemy.chains`:
  - Data per chain: anchor, gap, `stretch` (scale_sprite_x), `front` (z_index against the body), and one piece per link.
  - Drawing: `chainAt()` in `motion.ts`, then `drawChains()` in `CreatureSprite.tsx`. The chain hangs down, sways more toward the tip, and follows the body's bob.
  - Creatures covered: Kolmisilmä's tail and vines, Kätyri and Sauvojen tuntija tentacles, slime tentacles, Lonkero tentacles, Kolmisilmä parts' hair, and the wraith and monk capes.
  - Cloth (Mina's cape) has no sprites and is skipped.
- **Held and loose parts** now always join an animation, in their own default pose:
  - Mina's arm: its animations are named `default`/`down`, so the shared-name rule dropped it.
  - Unohdettu's pupil: a plain child sprite that `eye.lua` moves, drawn at the eye centre (`looseSprites`).
  - The monk's hands are left out on purpose. They are separate Lua-driven creatures with their own health.

## Toxic sludge
- The weakness is a permanent `GameEffectComponent effect="ALLERGY_RADIOACTIVE"` on a child entity, not a damage multiplier.
- The extractor now writes `Enemy.weakTo = ['Toxic sludge']` for the 19 creatures that have it. `goblin_bomb` was missing from the old hand-written list.
- Where it shows: How to beat it ("Weak to Toxic sludge: …") and a `Toxic → Weak` row in Damage taken. The wiki gives no number, so the row shows none.
- The hand-written family tip was removed, so the tip and the data can't drift apart.

## Notes
- The bestiary atlas is now 2049×2373. That size was updated in `public/game/version.json`, and the rest of the file was kept.
- The extract rewrote `public/game/fonts/*`; those changes were reverted.
- The Moves tile "Crawls" for Kolmisilmä is an existing curated wiki note (`MOVES` in enemyFights.ts). At the user's request it now reads "Flies" (3b675bc).
- Laser sights were hidden (`visible="0"` sprites are skipped), 3bb5d82.

# Range spell visuals (phases 0–7)
The user's research folder `opencode/noita_research/spell_visuals/` was checked against our code and data.wak.
- Most claims held up.
- The claim that "material alpha is dropped" was wrong: alpha was already kept.
- Megalaser's components really are disabled until `megalaser_launch.lua` switches them on.
- The post_glow shader math matched: about 0.942 of the glow kept each frame and 0.175 added.

The work, phase by phase:
- **1 (a26919c):**
  - Glow comes from `gfx_glow` as a strength. One helper serves both terrain and particles: `src/data/glow.ts`.
  - Switched-off components are kept and turned on by `enableParts`.
  - Every LaserEmitterComponent is read.
  - Image-emitter PNGs ship under `game/emitters/`. They used to be a 2.3 MB baked JSON; they're now baked at runtime.
- **2–3 (d47792a):**
  - Trails run from the last emit point.
  - `velocity_sets_scale` stretch, and lights at their real radius.
  - A ¼-res CPU port of post_glow1/2 (`glow.ts`), screen-blended. Taps are 1.5 world px apart, not 1.5 ¼-res texels.
- **4 (0682eb8):** laser beams are a particle scatter each frame (`beam.ts`), and cells wear down by `damage_to_cells` against their hp.
- **5 (a43f5a1, a8b953f):**
  - Image emitters (Circle, Cloud, Mist, Megalaser charge-up), `attractor_force` and Sea liquid.
  - Cell glow enters at gfx_glow/255. post_glow2's ×4 only undoes cell blitting's HDR squeeze.
  - a43f5a1 was pushed with a flaky test and its CI failed; a8b953f fixed the raytrace and the test.
- **6 (f4b3c3b):** `render_back` layers, MATERIAL_* colours, sprite variants and `next` animations, SpriteParticleEmitter looks, a smooth airflow field.
- **7 (this commit):**
  - post_final.frag lighting (`light.ts`): every game biome is dark, and lights and glow light the foreground.
  - The cave floor is an untinted 0.35, plus the player's 350 px light.
  - The Test cave (`range`, `sky: 1`) and performance mode look the same as before.
