## Round 2 (same day): Wand Builder rules
The user asked to validate the wand facts and put them in the builder.
- Read the full wiki Wands page from its raw wikitext (`/wiki/Wands?action=raw`), and checked the generation rules
  against `public/game/lua/data/scripts/gun/procedural/gun_procedural.lua`.
- The game file settled two conflicts on the wiki page:
  - The capacity clamp to 2–26 is skipped only when `Random(0,10000)` rolls 10000, so it's **1 in 10,001**, not 1 in 10,000.
  - The wiki's maximum (62 vs 66) couldn't be settled, so the note doesn't give one.
- Rules from the game file, not on the wiki:
  - Speed multiplier bands: ~2.4% faster (1–2), ~2.4% slower (0.5–1), ~1 in 2,100 up to 10.
  - Rare wands get +65 stat budget.
  - Forced no-shuffle chance is 15% + 6% per tier.
  - Leftover budget buys half the capacity on no-shuffle wands.
  - About 1 in 6 wands has a big mana pool with slow charge, about 1 in 7 a fast charger.
- Implementation:
  - New `MechanicKind` `'wand'`, with the notes under `wand:builder` in `src/data/mechanics.ts` (`wandRules()`).
  - It's rendered as `<HiddenMechanics kind="wand" id="builder" />` under Perks in `src/features/wand/WandPage.tsx`, behind the
    same "Show hidden mechanics" toggle as the other pages.
- `tests/mechanics.test.ts` checks that the clamp, the 1-second recharge rule and the rare roll still match gun_procedural.lua.
- Checked visually at 1440 px and 390 px (scratch script `wandrules.mts` against `vite preview --port 4173`). It renders cleanly.
